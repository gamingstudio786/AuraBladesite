Privacy Policy

How Aura Blade’s games and this website handle information, in plain language.

Last updated: October 15, 2026
On this page Who we are Information we process How we use it Ads Third-party services Sharing Children Your choices Retention and security Changes Contact

1. Who we are

Aura Blade (“we”, “us”) is a mobile game developer based in Lahore, Pakistan. This policy applies to our Android games, including Police Officer Border Sim Game (package com.aurablade.police.border.game), and to this website, aurablade.site.

By installing or using our games you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please uninstall the game.

2. Information we process

We do not ask you to create an account or enter your name to play. The information below is collected automatically by the game and by the third-party services built into it.

  • Device and app information: device model, operating system version, app version, language, and similar technical settings.
  • Identifiers: the Android advertising ID and other app or installation identifiers.
  • Approximate location: your IP address, from which a general location such as country or city can be inferred, mainly for ad delivery. The game does not read your precise GPS location.
  • Game activity: sessions, progress, levels, and how you interact with features, and with ads.
  • Diagnostics: crash logs and performance data that help us fix bugs.
  • Messages you send us: if you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include.

Our games are not designed to access your contacts, messages, call logs, camera, microphone, photos or files.

3. How we use it

  • To run the game and save your progress on your device.
  • To show ads that keep the game free, and to measure how those ads perform.
  • To understand how the game is used so we can improve it.
  • To find and fix crashes and technical problems.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse, and to meet legal obligations.
  • To reply to support and privacy requests.

4. Ads

Police Officer Border Sim Game is supported by ads served through Google AdMob. AdMob may use your advertising ID, IP address, device information and ad interactions to deliver, measure and limit ads, and to prevent fraud. Depending on your region and settings, the game may ask for your consent before showing personalized ads.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your Android settings (usually under Settings → Privacy → Ads). Learn more in Google’s advertising policy.

6. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with the service providers listed above, who process it to provide their services, and when required to comply with law, protect our rights or users, or as part of a business transfer such as a merger or sale of the game.

7. Children

Our games are rated for players aged 16 and over and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will take appropriate action.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Uninstall the game at any time. Progress stored on your device is removed with it.
  • Control ads by resetting your advertising ID or opting out of personalization in Android settings.
  • Ask us to access, correct or delete information we hold about you by emailing contact@aurablade.site. Because the game does not use accounts, we may need your device or advertising ID to find any data linked to you. Data held by Google or other providers is managed under their own policies.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law, such as objecting to or restricting processing, or withdrawing consent.

9. Retention and security

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law. Retention by third-party providers follows their own settings and policies. We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Our services may process information in countries other than your own, including through our service providers.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our games or legal requirements change. The latest version is always at aurablade.site/privacy.html, with the date above updated.
