Police Officer Border Sim Game

Inspect vehicles, verify passports and catch smugglers across a series of border missions. Free to play on Android.

About the game

What a shift looks like

No rush, no clutter. Just the small decisions that keep a border running.

Inspect vehicles

Check cars, buses and cargo trucks as they arrive. Open luggage, look closely and find what has been hidden.

Verify documents

Read passports, ID papers and driving licenses. Missing details and small mismatches tell you who may continue.

Spot smuggling

Notice what does not add up, search suspicious cargo and stop prohibited goods from crossing.
