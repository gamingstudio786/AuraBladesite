Your duties at the border

Start your day at the checkpoint and work through the traffic one vehicle at a time. Cars, buses and cargo trucks arrive for inspection, and each one needs your full attention.

Search luggage and cargo for hidden illegal goods. Check passports, identity papers and driving licenses for missing information and suspicious details. Then make the call: let them through, or stop them.

Missions get more demanding as you improve, so every shift gives you a new reason to stay sharp.

Features

  • Border patrol and police duty missions
  • Vehicle inspection and hidden contraband searches
  • Passport, ID and driving license checks
  • Smuggling detection challenges
