Our games may use third-party services for analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or other functionality. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Our services are not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review the request.

We take reasonable measures to help protect information associated with our services. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

Changes to This Policy
