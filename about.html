About Aura Blade

Building mobile games with care.

Aura Blade is a mobile game development and publishing brand focused on accessible, entertaining, and polished digital experiences.

Our approach

We combine game development, product iteration, creative production, store optimization, analytics, monetization, and player feedback to improve our games over time.

Player first

We aim to make controls understandable, gameplay enjoyable, and monetization clearly distinguishable from core gameplay.

Responsible data use

We seek to collect and use information only for legitimate product, advertising, analytics, security, support, and platform purposes relevant to a particular game.

Continuous improvement

Updates can include bug fixes, performance improvements, new content, balancing changes, and quality-of-life improvements.

Our games

Because Aura Blade publishes multiple mobile titles, individual games can differ in features, target audience, monetization, and third-party SDKs. The privacy practices applicable to a specific game are described in that game's disclosures and the current version of this policy.
